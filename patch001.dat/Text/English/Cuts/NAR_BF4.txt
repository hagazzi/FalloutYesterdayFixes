1:Convinced that water and brahmin were the key to their tribe's prosperity, the Blackfoots began a tentative trade in water and animals with nearby settlements. Their ready supply of the precious drink and hardy stock were well received by their neighbors, and all villagers reaped the reward.
2:In time, the Blackfoots abandoned slaving for their new trade, and their village experienced a boom in wealth and trade. Though never fully trusted by other settlements, the Blackfoot gained a reputation for fairness and quality goods.
